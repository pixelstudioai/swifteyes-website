Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about the SwiftRise event management platform.

SwiftRise is an all-in-one event management platform built by Verto Technologies in Riyadh. It brings planning, registration, ticketing, on-site check-in, badge printing, exhibitor management, live agendas, networking, and real-time analytics into a single connected system — with dedicated iOS and Android apps for attendees and organizers.

SwiftRise is built for on-site, high-impact events: multi-day conferences and summits, exhibitions and trade shows, corporate and enterprise events, and large-scale government and public events. It is trusted by organizations across Saudi Arabia including ZATCA, Hewar Group, and SAMI.
