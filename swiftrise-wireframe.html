Built for the Reality of On-Site Events

SwiftRise is designed to handle the operational complexity of physical events, ensuring speed, accuracy, and control where it matters most — on the ground.

Faster, Smoother On-Site Operations

SwiftRise streamlines on-site execution by enabling quick attendee processing, reducing queues, and minimizing manual effort through optimized badge handling and self-service touchpoints. This allows event teams to maintain steady flow at entry points and focus on managing the experience rather than resolving operational bottlenecks.

  • Average check-in under 10 seconds — proven at ZATCA Conference 2024
  • On-site PVC badge printing with encoded chips
  • Self-service kiosks and multi-gate QR, RFID & NFC entry

Accurate Visibility and Crowd Insights

With real-time attendance data and advanced tracking capabilities, SwiftRise gives organizers a clear picture of crowd movement and on-site activity. These insights help teams monitor capacity, respond quickly to issues, and make informed decisions that improve safety, efficiency, and overall event performance.

  • Mesh network of AI-powered thermal cameras and LiDAR
  • Live occupancy counts and people-flow heatmaps by zone
  • Face-detection entry and real-time gate monitoring

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about the SwiftRise event management platform.

SwiftRise is an all-in-one event management platform built for organizers. It brings event planning, booth layouts, registration, ticketing, on-site check-in, live agendas, and attendee networking into a single dashboard. Created by Verto Technologies in Riyadh, SwiftRise helps teams run conferences, exhibitions, and corporate events from one connected system.

SwiftRise works in four steps: set up your event details and branding, manage exhibitors and attendees with invitations and RSVPs, design booth layouts on an interactive floor plan, then open registration and ticketing. On event day, attendees check in via QR codes while organizers track live attendance from a real-time dashboard.

SwiftRise is designed for event organizers, exhibition managers, conference planners, and corporate teams who coordinate registration, exhibitors, and attendees at scale. It suits trade shows, conferences, gala dinners, and internal company events, and is trusted by organizations across Saudi Arabia, including ZATCA, Hewar Group, and SAMI.

SwiftRise offers a real-time analytics dashboard, customizable landing pages, registration and ticketing, QR-based on-site check-in, interactive booth layouts, live streaming, polls and surveys, and multi-platform mobile apps.
